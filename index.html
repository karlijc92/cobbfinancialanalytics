FAQ

Common questions.

What information do I need to provide?

After purchase, you'll get a short intake form. Typically this includes basic financials (income statement, balance sheet if available), a short description of your business, and your specific goal for the analysis. If you don't have formal financials yet, that's okay — let me know in the intake form.

Do you provide investment advice or manage money?

No. This service is limited to financial analysis, modeling, and forecasting for your own business decisions. It is not personalized investment advice, securities recommendations, tax preparation, or audit services.

What if my business is more complex than the listed packages?

Starting prices reflect a typical small business or startup engagement. If your business has more complexity, I'll follow up after purchase to confirm final scope and pricing before starting any work.

Can I get a custom package?

Yes. Email with your situation and we can scope something custom if none of the three packages fit.

What industries do you work with?

I work across healthcare, nonprofit, technology/SaaS, and general small business. I have specific depth in nonprofit and community healthcare finance from 10+ years of hands-on experience.
